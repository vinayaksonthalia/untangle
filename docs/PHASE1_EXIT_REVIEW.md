## Decision

The implemented Phase 1 hardening scope passes its executable correctness, determinism,
coverage, payload, concurrency, cleanup, and claim-honesty gates. Phase 2 may be planned next,
but Phase 1 is not represented as unconditionally complete: the dedicated SBI ingestion adapter
remains blocked until a sanitized authentic SBI export fixture is supplied and its provenance is
recorded.

No authentic SBI fixture was present during this review. Synthetic SBI-style narration is useful
benchmark coverage, but it is not evidence that native SBI statement ingestion works. The review
therefore did not add an `sbi_csv` adapter or change the evidence level in
`BANK_FORMAT_EVIDENCE.md`.
