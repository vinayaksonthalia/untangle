The Indian ingestion trap

Why controllers lose weeks reconciling current accounts

Hundreds of lump-sum credits pool into one HDFC/ICICI/Axis current account every morning. When channels commingle, ordinary tools break down.

1

Razorpay T+2 batch settlements

A single lumped credit represents hundreds of checkouts minus card MDR, UPI interchange, and 18% GST withheld — matching by amount is impossible.

Risk: unextracted GST ITC.
2

Direct NEFT / RTGS payouts

B2B distributor credits intermingle with gateway payouts on the same date; fuzzy matchers pair them because amounts coincide.

Risk: distorted debtor aging.
3

Direct UPI & QR collections

Truncated narrations without invoice identifiers flood the statement with micro-transactions.

Risk: duplicate revenue recognition.
4

Loan drawdowns & sweeps

Term loans, sweep interest, and overdraft fees share the account; tools mistake sweeps for settlements.

Risk: debt booked as revenue.
5

Chargeback clawbacks

A dispute docks a later, unrelated settlement; heuristics miss the negative deduction and the ledger drifts.

Risk: unrecognised liabilities.
Why precision matters

The engine

Four rules untangle never breaks

Invariant 01

Integer-paise arithmetic

Floating point leaks fractions of paise. untangle converts every amount to exact integer paise, conserving money to the paisa.

Gross − (MDR + GST + refunds) ≡ net bank credit (residual = 0 paise)
Invariant 02

Abstain, never equivocate

If two settlements share a net total without a distinct UTR, untangle refuses to guess — it isolates the candidates for a human.

Zero false positives: 5 honest abstentions > 1 bogus auto-match
Invariant 03

Exact subset-sum decomposition

When a payout splits or aggregates orders, untangle proves a unique combination satisfies the net amount — no random combinatorial ambiguity.

Constrained integer partition, verified — not approximated
Invariant 04

Hash-bound audit trail

Every run carries a content SHA-256 audit root and per-packet proof checks — re-derive the hash in any browser. Signed with ECDSA P-256 when a key is configured, else hash-bound.

Re-hashable, tamper-evident — not a Merkle-tree claim we don't make

Safer by design

Read-only toward money

We state exactly what is guaranteed, and what is not. No certifications we don't hold.

Zero write access

Files are processed in memory and discarded when the session ends. No application database stores your statements; nothing is sent to a third party.
