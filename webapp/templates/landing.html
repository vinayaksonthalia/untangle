Why It Matters

Automated integrity for forensic accountants and financial controllers.

The 5% that eats 80% of the work

60–75% of settlement lines auto-match on day one. It's the mangled UTRs, split settlements, and cross-cycle refunds — barely 1–4% of lines — that consume 70–80% of a finance team's time. untangle is built for that slice.

It refuses to guess

In India a wrong or unexplained credit can attract ~78% tax (Section 115BBE). untangle only calls a credit Razorpay's when the settlement report proves it, and abstains otherwise. Precision is a financial safeguard.

How it works

1

UPLOAD

Upload your bank statements and order ledgers securely into a temporary processing sandbox.

2

ATTRIBUTE

Match credits via deterministic UTR logic. Razorpay only on a genuine tie. No tie → abstain.

3

RECONCILE

Verify the evidence-backed slice. Decompose gross → fee → GST → net to bank.

4

INVESTIGATE

Agentic root-cause trace for exceptions surfaces actionable insights for unmatched lines.

5

EXPORT & VERIFY

Export balanced journals and close certificates. Re-verify independently.
