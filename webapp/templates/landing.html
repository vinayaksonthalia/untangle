Safer by design

Read-only toward money.

What is guaranteed, and what is not — stated plainly.

Zero write access

untangle ingests read-only statements. There is no code path to move, authorise or debit funds, and it never posts to your books.

Privacy by construction

Files are processed in memory and discarded when the session ends. Results stay in your own browser tab; no database stores your statements.

No overclaims

No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or statutory certification is claimed. Benchmarks are synthetic unless named. See SAFETY.md.
