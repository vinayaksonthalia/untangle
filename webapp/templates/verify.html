Independent verification

Verify a close certificate

Paste any untangle close certificate below. This page re-runs the same proof logic your browser can inspect: it recomputes the certificate's content hash and compares it to the claimed hash, checks the signature if one is present, and reports the proof-packet binding — so you can confirm the reconciliation results are byte-for-byte unaltered since issuance. Nothing is uploaded or stored; verification runs on the certificate you paste.

Don't trust. Verify. The verdict is deterministic and reproducible.
