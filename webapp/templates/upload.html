Upload your files

Three files: your bank statement, the Razorpay settlement report (in untangle's expected schema), and your order ledger. They're sent to untangle's /reconcile endpoint, reconciled and discarded when your report renders — not persisted to any database and never sent to a third party. (A large multipart upload may be briefly spooled to a temporary file by the server, which is removed when the request ends.) Results are saved in this browser tab, not in a server run database. Use “Clear this tab’s results” on a shared device; browser session restore or duplicating a tab may preserve a copy. Large completed results download as JSON instead of opening the dashboard. If tab storage is unavailable, a download is offered so your completed result is not lost.

REQUIRED

Bank statement

Credits from your current account, as a CSV.

REQUIRED

Razorpay settlement report

The settlement recon report in untangle's expected JSON schema — the join key type + entity_id, plus fee, tax, settled_at, settlement_utr… (untangle's schema, not a raw provider export).

REQUIRED

Order ledger

Your order manifest (order_id, amount_paise, status), as a CSV.
