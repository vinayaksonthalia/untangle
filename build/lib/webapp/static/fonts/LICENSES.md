# Self-hosted font licenses

The landing page serves these fonts from `/static/fonts/` because the app's
hardened Content-Security-Policy (`default-src 'self'`) forbids loading fonts or
stylesheets from external hosts (e.g. fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com).

All three families are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1**,
which permits redistribution and self-hosting (including bundling with an
application) provided the copyright/license notices below are retained and the
fonts are not sold on their own.

Files are the `latin` and `latin-ext` woff2 subsets exported by the Google Fonts
API, regenerated by `tools/tailwind/fetch_fonts.py`.
