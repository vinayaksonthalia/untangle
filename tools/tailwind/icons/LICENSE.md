`material-design-icons` project, fetched from the official Google Fonts icon
endpoint by `tools/tailwind/fetch` step and inlined into the landing page by
`tools/tailwind/build_landing.py`.

Each icon is used verbatim (path data unchanged); only presentational attributes
(`fill="currentColor"`, `width`/`height="1em"`, `aria-hidden`) are applied at
inline time. No modifications are made to the glyph geometry.
